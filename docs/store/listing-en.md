# Google Play store listing (English, en-US)

**App name (30 chars)**
Ginkgo Avenue: The Last Leaf

**Short description (80 chars)**
Save the golden ginkgo avenue in 3D. How many trees can you keep for 7 days?

**Full description**
A straight avenue of 146 ginkgo trees runs toward a domed gallery in a Tokyo park.
The golden leaves are at their peak, but every gust of wind strips them away.

When a tree starts to wither, a glowing orb appears on it — at its roots, at its top, or halfway up the trunk.
Run, jump and climb to touch the orb, and the tree's autumn colors come back.
Hop from treetop to treetop to reach them faster.

- Keep the last leaf from falling through the 7-day ginkgo festival (one day = one minute)
- Your score is the number of trees that still have leaves at the end of day 7
- Each tree can be revived only once every two days, so keep moving
- Watch out for cars on the road

Features a carpet of golden leaves, day and night with an evening light-up, and an original music-box soundtrack.
