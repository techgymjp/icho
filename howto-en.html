Late autumn in a Tokyo park

Ginkgo AvenueThe Last Leaf

How to play

What is this game?

A straight avenue of 146 ginkgo trees runs from Aoyama-dori to the Meiji Memorial Picture Gallery in Meiji Jingu Gaien, Tokyo. The golden leaves are at their peak, but every gust of cold wind strips more of them away.

When a tree starts to wither, a glowing orb appears on it. That is the sign. Touch the orb and the tree turns golden again. The orb is at the roots, at the top, or halfway up the trunk. Jump or climb to reach it. Healthy trees have no orb.

Goal and game over

Keep the last leaf from falling through the 7-day ginkgo festival (one day = one minute) to clear the game. Your score is the number of trees that still have leaves at the end of day 7 (out of 146). The amount of leaves left on the whole avenue (compared with its peak) is shown too.
  • When every tree has lost all its leaves — the last leaf falls and winter comes.
  • When you run into a moving car — cars keep driving along the avenue and Aoyama-dori.

Each tree can be revived only once every two days. If a tree you revived starts to wither again on the same day or the next day, no orb appears, because it cannot be revived yet. Two days later it can be revived again (for example, a tree revived on day 1 can be revived again from day 3). Keep moving between trees.

Controls

Walk / runWASD or the arrow keys (↑ moves forward). Hold to speed up. Shift to run
Phone: the stick at the bottom left
CameraThe camera follows about 8 m behind you. Move the mouse to turn it (the cursor is hidden while playing; Esc pauses)
Phone: drag on the right side of the screen
JumpPress Space to jump at once. Keep holding it to rise higher — at full power you reach the treetops.
Phone: hold the Jump button
ClimbMove toward a trunk to climb it, and away from it to climb down. Hold to climb faster. You can jump off while climbing.
RideYou can stand on trees and on car roofs. Ride a moving car and it carries you along. You can jump from there, too.
MusicA music-box waltz plays. Turn it on or off with the M key (or the button on the title and pause screens).
Catch leavesWhen a falling leaf is near you (the circle in the center lights up), click (on a phone, tap anywhere except the stick). A golden leaf counts as 10.

A day on the avenue

  • One day passes every minute. In the evening the low sun shines through the trees, and at night the light-up and street lamps come on.
  • The more leaves the avenue has, and the brighter the day, the more people stroll along it. Late at night and early in the morning it is almost empty.
  • Each tree loses its leaves at its own pace, and a cold wind makes them fall all at once.
  • On Aoyama-dori, cross at the crosswalk while the traffic light is red for cars.

Tips

  • An orb glows gold when a tree starts to wither and blinks red when fewer than 30% of its leaves are left. The minimap at the top right shows thin trees in red.
  • You can step over the low fences onto the road. Watch out for cars.
  • Jump from the roof of a parked car to get a little higher.
  • On a treetop, hold a direction and press Space to hop to the next treetop that way. Keep pressing to hop along the avenue.
  • The bottom left shows the fps (frames per second) and the quality level. If the game runs slowly, the quality is lowered automatically. To start in light mode, add ?q=low to the end of the URL.
